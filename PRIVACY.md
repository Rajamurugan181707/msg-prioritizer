# Msg Prioritizer: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Msg Prioritizer ranks your incoming messages by importance. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

## What the app reads

With your permission (Android's **Notification access**), the app reads the notifications shown by these messaging apps: WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Telegram X, Signal, Google Messages and Samsung Messages. From each notification it uses:

- the sender's name or number
- the chat or group name
- the message text
- the time it arrived
- whether you tapped or swiped away the notification, so it can learn who matters to you

It ignores notifications from every other app.

## Where your data goes

**Nowhere.** All processing happens on your phone.

- The app does not request internet permission, so it **cannot** send data off your device.
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party services.
- Messages are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and are **deleted automatically after 7 days**.
- What the app learns about your contacts (how often you open or swipe away their notifications) is stored only on your phone. You can erase it at any time with **Settings → Forget what it learned**.
- The app is excluded from Android cloud backups.

## Your control

- Turn off **Notification access** at any time in Android Settings, and the app stops reading notifications immediately.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored.
- The app never sends, deletes or changes messages in your messaging apps. It only reads notifications.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because nothing leaves the device, no personal data is collected by the developer at all.
